## Snippets (paper-ready prose)

**Purpose:** Carefully worded sentences/paragraphs for later drafting. Prefer these over improvised strong language.  
**Rules:** Report what the corpus shows; ambiguity → worse/more conservative tier. Non-specificity of controls ≠ “nothing infectious exists.”

### R2-S1 — What Tier A means for the paper

> A rigorous Isolation-stage negative control is a culture treated like the Isolation condition—including **matched maintenance/serum enrichment**—but **without** the viral inoculum. Controls that omit that match, or that exist only at a different stage, cannot audit whether CPE under Isolation conditions is virus-specific.

### R2-S2 — Scope of the scoring result

> We score Isolation-stage culture controls as reported in the paper corpus (tiers A–D). Absence or mismatch of controls is a **methods quality** finding about Isolation practice publications; it does not by itself prove that CPE is non-specific in every laboratory system.
