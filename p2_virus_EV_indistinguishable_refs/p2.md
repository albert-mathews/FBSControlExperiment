**Purpose:** Carefully worded sentences/paragraphs for later drafting. Prefer these over improvised strong language.  
**Rules:** Prediction from Isolation under-determination, not “viruses do not exist.” Quote-backed EV literature when claiming field consensus. Update when wording is refined.

### P2-S1 — Hinge (from `grok.md`; keep)

> EV indistinguishability is not a late technical annoyance — it is what you would expect if the “second particle class” (virions) was never observationally grounded in the first place.

### P2-S2 — Scope guardrail

> Physical co-purification and morphological overlap of culture-derived particles with EVs do not by themselves prove that no infectious agent exists. They show that **Isolation → purify → EM/biochem** does not uniquely define a second particle class under the conditions that produced the Isolate.

### P2-S3 — Multi-metric field problem (provisional; refine after PDF freeze)

> Across size, buoyant density, ultracentrifugation, precipitation, and often chromatography, culture-derived membrane nanoparticles attributed to virions occupy the same operational windows as host extracellular vesicles; field methods literature treats complete physical separation as non-guaranteed rather than routine.

### P2-S4 — Historical non-novelty (provisional; needs 1997 full-text quotes locked)

> Recognition that membrane microvesicles co-purify with gradient-enriched virus preparations is already explicit in late-1990s HIV purification work; later EV–virus reviews extend that problem rather than inventing it.
